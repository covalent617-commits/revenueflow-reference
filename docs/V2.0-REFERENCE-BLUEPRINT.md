# RevenueFlow V2.0 — Master Reference Blueprint

**Status:** Approved product direction and reference-site specification  
**Reference implementation branch:** `v2.0-full-reference`  
**Purpose:** Make the complete product vision understandable and provide a coherent design/implementation target. This document does not claim that the production application already implements these capabilities.

---

## 1. Product definition

RevenueFlow is one connected business revenue and operations platform. It detects revenue leakage, explains evidence, prioritises risk, coordinates interventions and measures outcomes. It connects commercial operations, customer success, project delivery, finance, automation, documents and executive intelligence around shared business records.

The product must not feel like a collection of disconnected mini-applications. A user should be able to move from a risk signal to its affected customer/opportunity, inspect evidence, take an authorised action, track its completion and outcome, then return to the original investigation without losing context.

### Product truth rules

- Booked revenue, invoiced revenue, collected cash, open pipeline, weighted forecast, profit, exposure and recovered revenue are distinct measures.
- A hypothetical simulator output is never represented as actual revenue.
- A risk score is an explainable signal, not proof of loss.
- Silence alone does not prove a deal is lost.
- A quote view is not acceptance; acceptance is not necessarily a completed signature.
- A recommendation is not an outcome. Track accepted, completed, ignored, unsuccessful and measured interventions.
- Claim recovered/protected revenue only when the intervention and subsequent business outcome support attribution.
- Deterministic calculations and core CRM operations work without AI. AI cannot invent facts, override authoritative calculations or bypass permissions.

## 2. Eight product divisions

### Division 1 — Identity, Organisation and Access

- One user may belong to multiple organisations and switch without logging out.
- Membership, data, analytics, notifications, integrations, documents and intelligence are tenant-isolated.
- Support Owner, Admin, Manager and Member roles plus custom granular roles.
- Personal profile/job title is separate from organisation role.
- Invitations have Pending, Accepted, Expired and Revoked states. Only accepted membership grants access.
- Secure email verification, password recovery, session revocation and audit history.
- Permission checks cover navigation, dashboard widgets, records, fields, actions, exports, assistant context and external sharing.
- Record scopes include assigned, team and all-organisation records.
- A padlock communicates role-permission or subscription-plan restrictions. If both apply, explain both. Backend enforcement is mandatory; the lock is UX only and must not leak protected data.

### Division 2 — Lead, Sales and Customer Operations

Workspaces: Leads; Opportunities; Customers & Contacts; Products & Services; Quotes & Proposals; Contracts; Approval Centre; Competitor Intelligence.

- Lead sources, qualification, routing, ownership, conversion and history.
- Opportunities, configurable pipelines, stages, values, probability, close dates, next actions and outcome reasons.
- Customer 360 links contacts, opportunities, quotes, projects, invoices, communications, service cases, health and renewals.
- Product/service catalogue supports recurring retainers, one-off work, units, prices, discounts and tax rules.
- Quote/proposal line items, quantities, pricing, discounts, taxes, totals, validity, terms, versions, approvals, PDF, branded sharing and status history.
- Contract lifecycle, obligations, versions, approvals, renewal dates and signing-provider handoff.
- Shared approval engine covers discounts, pricing, quotes, proposals, contracts, expenses, invoice adjustments and high-value project changes.
- Competitor intelligence is a dedicated management workspace under Intelligence and a specialised view within Leak Radar Sales Intelligence. It uses shared signal records, not duplicate risk cases.

### Division 3 — Project and Service Delivery

Workspaces: Projects & Delivery; Resource Capacity; Customer Success & Renewals; Tasks & Milestones; Customer Support.

- A won deal can create a delivery project with onboarding, milestones, tasks/subtasks, dependencies, deliverables, owners, deadlines, resource allocation, capacity, budget and cost tracking.
- Customer-visible progress is explicitly scoped.
- MAPs are pre-sale buyer/seller commitments; projects are post-sale delivery. They are distinct records and workflows.
- Support cases include assignment, priority, status, comments, attachments, escalation, response and resolution tracking.
- Project budgets and cost variances use defined calculations; do not label unaudited figures as audited profit.

### Division 4 — Financial Operations

Workspaces: CFO Dashboard; Invoices & Receivables; Expenses & Costs; Commissions.

- Invoice lifecycle: Draft → Approved → Issued → Partially Paid → Paid, with Overdue, Disputed, Void and Written Off states.
- Payment allocation, collections, ageing and reconciliation.
- Supplier expenses and direct project costs; budget, cost variance and profitability.
- Commission plans, eligibility, calculation basis, adjustment, approval and payout status.
- CFO Dashboard includes booked revenue, cash collected, receivables, expenses, costs, margins, forecast, pipeline, project costs, commissions, customer financial health, renewals and revenue exposure.
- Every metric has a definition, scope, time period and drill-down to supporting records.
- Financial visibility is permission-controlled. Full accounting/general ledger is deferred.

### Division 5 — Customer Success and Growth

Workspaces: Customer Success & Renewals; Customer Portal; Mutual Action Plans.

- Onboarding, adoption, success plans, health signals, support trends, renewals, expansion and retention interventions.
- Health indicators are evidence-backed operational views, not guarantees.
- Customer portal is one branded dashboard with secure direct links to individual resources.
- Portal resources may include quotes, proposals, documents, MAPs, project milestones and customer interactions.
- External users see only explicitly shared resources; never internal CRM, private notes or other customers.
- Resource-specific links support expiry, revocation, access logging, rate limits and configurable verification/OTP.
- Sensible defaults apply; sensitive documents and legally significant actions require stronger verification where appropriate.
- MAPs support buyer-owned, seller-owned and jointly owned milestones, due dates, comments, permitted documents, questions, uploads and change requests.

### Division 6 — Automation and Integration

Workspaces: Email & Communications; Meeting Scheduling; Automation & Workflows; Integrations & API.

- One configurable workflow engine: triggers, conditions, actions, delays, branches, approvals, escalations, retries, execution logs and version history.
- Ghost-Buster is a workflow/use case on shared infrastructure, not a separate engine.
- Email execution is contextual, not a general email client. Compose from opportunities, customers, Leak Radar, quotes, timelines and AI. Include drafts, templates, personalisation, attachments, sender selection, preview, send confirmation, delivery/failure states where providers expose them and communication-to-record association.
- Email signals include unanswered after configurable intervals, promised-but-unsent follow-up, unanswered customer questions, repeated unanswered outreach, response received and task completed. Opens are unreliable and must be treated cautiously.
- Scheduling supports individual and shared team booking pages, availability, duration, buffers, notice, horizon, timezone, location/video, conflict rules, rescheduling and cancellation.
- Integrate Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 rather than rebuilding calendar infrastructure. Prevent double booking and protect private event details.
- OAuth, least-privilege scopes, encrypted tokens, revocation, deduplication, sync state, retries, errors and audit are required.
- External sends and consequential actions require explicit confirmation unless an organisation configures a narrowly scoped approved automation.

### Division 7 — Document and Data Management

Workspaces: Documents & Data; Import Centre.

- Shared document service with private storage, resource-level access, versions, safe sharing and retention controls.
- Validate MIME/content, apply malware scanning where available, safe processing and resource-scoped uploads.
- CSV/Excel imports include field mapping, validation, duplicate detection, preview, dry-run, error report and import history.
- Exports honour tenant and role permissions.
- OCR/AI extraction requires human verification. Do not silently create authoritative financial or contractual records from uncertain extraction.

### Division 8 — Unified Business Intelligence

Workspaces: Leak Radar; Analytics & Forecast; Executive Business Command Centre; Revenue Recovery Simulator.

- Leak Radar has exactly three primary tabs: Sales Intelligence, Customer Intelligence and Process Intelligence.
- Lead Intelligence and Competitor Intelligence are specialised Sales Intelligence views, not additional Leak Radar primary tabs.
- One Unified Revenue Risk Engine powers all three tabs. Risk cases include evidence, affected records, defensible exposure, severity, owner, timeline, recommendations, investigation state, resolution history and measured outcome.
- Risk lifecycle: Detected → Investigating → Action Initiated → Resolved / Escalated / Dismissed → Outcome Measured.
- Deduplicate shared exposure; version rules; configurable thresholds; idempotency; tenant isolation; monitoring.
- Analytics report library:
  1. Revenue Intelligence
  2. Pipeline Performance
  3. Win/Loss Analysis
  4. Follow-up Performance
  5. Leak Radar Intelligence
  6. Customer Intelligence
  7. Quote Performance
  8. Team Performance
  9. Forecast Analysis
  10. Competitor Intelligence
  11. Process Performance
  12. Intervention Outcomes
- Executive Business Command Centre connects finance, pipeline, forecast, delivery, customers, renewals, team capacity, commissions, exposure and intervention outcomes.
- Revenue Recovery Simulator lives in Analytics, with a prominent CFO Dashboard summary and shortcut. Formula: affected value × assumed recovery probability × intervention effectiveness. Controls update the hypothetical scenario only. Include baseline comparison, reset, save/compare/export and keyboard alternatives in the production implementation.

## 3. Navigation and workspace architecture

The reference uses grouped navigation rather than eight separate division menus:

- **Home:** Dashboard; Attention Queue; CFO Dashboard; Executive Business Command Centre.
- **CRM / Commercial:** Leads; Opportunities; Customers & Contacts; Quotes & Proposals; Products & Services; Contracts; Approval Centre.
- **Intelligence:** Leak Radar; Forecast; Analytics.
- **Delivery / Collaboration:** Projects & Delivery; Resource Capacity; Customer Success & Renewals; Tasks & Milestones; Customer Support; Mutual Action Plans; Customer Portal.
- **Finance:** Invoices & Receivables; Expenses & Costs; Commissions.
- **Execution / Platform:** Email & Communications; Meeting Scheduling; Automation & Workflows; Documents & Data; Records; Import Centre; Integrations & API.
- **Administration:** Team Performance; Activity & Audit; Settings; Notifications.

Lead Intelligence and Competitor Intelligence are specialised Sales Intelligence views within Leak Radar. They are not additional primary Leak Radar tabs and do not create duplicate risk records. A contextual competitor analysis view may open from Sales Intelligence. The customer portal is an external experience, not an internal CRM role.

The original operational workspaces remain distinct: Dashboard is a personal summary; Attention Queue is the actionable task/approval/follow-up queue; Forecast is forward-looking projections; Analytics is historical and operational exploration; Leak Radar investigates evidence-backed risks; Activity is chronological history; Records is a searchable supporting-record index; Notifications are personal system alerts; Team Performance covers workload and outcomes; Settings owns organisation configuration and access administration.

**Leak Radar has exactly three primary tabs:** Sales Intelligence, Customer Intelligence and Process Intelligence.

## 4. Dashboard and role experiences

All dashboard layouts are role-aware and user-customisable by rearranging permitted widgets. The reference supports drag-and-drop widget ordering. Production layouts must be saved per user and only render widgets/data permitted by the active organisation membership and record scope.

### Identity model — non-negotiable

1. A **user account** represents an individual person.
2. An **organisation** is a business entity/workspace. It never has an employee role.
3. A **membership** connects an individual user to an organisation and contains that person's role, permissions, data scope and membership status.
4. One person may hold different roles in different organisations.
5. The organisation owner or an authorised administrator invites members and assigns roles. An invited person cannot select, elevate or self-assign their role.
6. Role management, invitations, custom roles, permission configuration, member-role assignment and access history belong in **Settings**.
7. The top-bar persona selector in the reference is explicitly a demo-only preview control; it is not a production role switcher or permission editor.

- **Owner:** protected ownership powers, governance and organisation-wide oversight.
- **Admin:** organisation configuration, invitations and delegated role assignment; finance data remains permission-filtered.
- **Sales Manager:** pipeline, team activity, opportunity risk, follow-ups, quote performance and customer health.
- **Finance Manager:** receivables, cash, expenses, costs, margins, commissions, CFO metrics and financial exceptions.
- **Operations Manager:** projects, delivery, resource capacity, budgets, support escalations and customer commitments.
- **Customer Success Manager:** customer health, adoption, support history, renewals and retention interventions.
- **Project Manager:** assigned project delivery, milestones, budgets and capacity coordination.
- **Sales Representative / Member:** assigned commercial records, tasks, communications and permitted risk context.

CFO Dashboard is a true executive financial command centre, not another analytics report. The complete CFO design is visible in the reference preview; production access remains server-side permission-controlled.

## 5. Global interaction patterns

- Persistent organisation switcher near the product identity; switching changes the complete tenant context.
- Global search across permitted records.
- Context-preserving detail views and cross-links between related records.
- Right-side email composer by default; expand to full workspace for longer composition.
- Persistent draggable/resizable AI assistant on desktop, compact/expanded preference remembered; mobile bottom sheet or full-screen.
- AI answers cite or link to authorised supporting records. Confirm before sending messages, approving contracts, making payments, deleting records or other consequential actions.
- Clear loading, empty, error, restricted-access and success states.
- No decorative controls that imply functionality. In the reference, external provider actions are clearly marked as previews and do not execute.

## 5A. Visual identity, charts and workspace personality

Preserve the deep navy foundation and layered blue surfaces while restoring the original richer RevenueFlow semantic palette:

- Green / teal: positive performance, successful outcomes, collections and improvement.
- Amber / orange: warnings, attention and pending action.
- Red / coral: critical risks, losses, overdue items and negative outcomes.
- Blue / cyan: informational states, active selections and analytical distinctions.
- Purple: secondary analytical categories and specialised intelligence.

Use semantic colours consistently and pair colour with labels/icons so meaning is never colour-only. Do not use random gradients or indiscriminate neon accents.

Each workspace keeps its own information architecture and visual personality. Dashboard is personal and prioritised; Opportunities is pipeline/deal-stage focused; Customers is relationship and history focused; Forecast is actual-versus-expected forward projection; Analytics is historical and operational exploration; Leak Radar is evidence/severity/investigation focused; CFO Dashboard is executive financial command; Tasks and Attention are execution-focused; Team is workload/capacity focused; Settings is restrained configuration.

### Leak Radar circular visual

The circular visual is a severity distribution, not an undefined composite risk score. It displays active case counts by Critical, High and Medium severity and must show the counts/labels beside the ring. Do not combine unrelated financial exposure, severity and investigation status into one score. It complements, and never replaces, the evidence-ranked risk lists and investigation detail. Keep exactly three primary tabs: Sales Intelligence, Customer Intelligence and Process Intelligence.

### Chart standards

Every chart identifies the measure, period, date labels, unit/currency, series, actual/forecast/simulation status and comparison basis. Line charts show visible points and connected series; hover and keyboard focus reveal exact date, series and value. Use appropriate chart forms: line/area for time trends; horizontal/grouped bars for categorical comparisons such as revenue by salesperson, stage counts and won/lost by month; funnel for pipeline conversion; donut or horizontal bars for composition; stacked bars for cash/receivables; ranked bars for risk severity; and progress/timelines for project delivery. Never replace a categorical comparison with a line chart merely for visual consistency. Won is green/teal; lost is red/coral; actual revenue is green/teal; forecast is blue/cyan; target is amber; negative variance is red/coral. Simulation must remain visibly hypothetical. Analytics report detail views must use report-specific measures and visualisations rather than a generic placeholder chart or financial KPI repeated across unrelated reports. Revenue and forecast reports show separately labelled currency series; win/loss and customer-health reports show labelled distributions; pipeline, risk, quote, competitor, team and intervention reports use count-based ranked/funnel visuals; follow-up and process reports use percentage or duration trends with matching units. Currency labels, chart legends, axis titles and tooltips must follow the active organisation display currency; changing the symbol must never imply foreign-exchange conversion.

## 5B. Dashboard personalisation and preview controls

Dashboard widgets may be rearranged by drag and drop in the reference. The signed-in profile displays the individual's assigned role as read-only information. There is no role selector or demo-persona control in the dashboard, profile menu or top navigation. The workspace switcher lists only organisations with an accepted membership for the signed-in individual and displays that membership's assigned role as read-only context. Selecting a workspace changes the active organisation, navigation, data, permission scope and AI context; it never changes the assigned role or grants permissions. A person may have different roles in different organisations. Settings → Members and Invitations → Roles and Permissions is the only role-administration area, visible only to authorised owners or administrators. Invitations are assigned a role by the inviter; only acceptance creates an active membership. The organisation itself never has a role.

## 6. Connected commercial lifecycle

Lead → Opportunity → Quote / Proposal → Approval → Customer Acceptance / Signature → Onboarding → Project Delivery → Invoice → Payment → Customer Success → Renewal.

Each transition links to authoritative records and preserves history. A won deal can initiate onboarding and project delivery. Delivery costs and invoice events inform financial views. Customer success and support inform health and renewal intelligence. Risk recommendations create reviewed tasks/workflows, and outcomes feed intervention measurement.

## 7. Shared architecture boundaries

Prefer a modular monolith before microservices. Reuse shared services rather than creating duplicate sources of truth:

- Identity, organisation, membership and session service.
- Server-side permission and record-scope service.
- Shared customer, contact, lead, opportunity, quote, contract, project, invoice and task records.
- Communication and calendar provider adapters.
- Shared document/attachment service.
- Shared approval engine.
- Shared workflow engine and durable asynchronous jobs.
- Notification service and append-oriented audit trail.
- Unified risk engine.
- Permission-aware AI orchestration and action-confirmation layer.
- Integration credentials, webhook, sync, retry and revocation services.

Preserve the current application stack where sound; do not rewrite without repository inspection. PostgreSQL, the existing ORM/migration framework, durable job queue, private object storage, secure sessions and OAuth adapters are architecture candidates, not claims about current production implementation.

## 8. Security, privacy and reliability requirements

- Enforce tenant isolation and server-side authorisation for every request and background job.
- Do not rely on hidden UI controls as security.
- No self-elevation; accepted membership only; revoke sessions and provider tokens.
- Minimise OAuth scopes and protect credentials.
- Secure external links with expiry, revocation, access logging, rate limiting and optional OTP.
- Keep internal notes and private customer information outside external portal payloads.
- Audit consequential actions, approvals, access changes, external shares and AI-initiated actions.
- Use idempotency and deduplication for provider sync and risk generation.
- Use durable jobs for sync, document processing and reminders; expose failures and retry state.
- Preserve record versions, lifecycle transitions and resolution history.

## 9. Reference-site status language

The reference must distinguish:
- **Core V1:** existing capability represented in the current V1 product, subject to current implementation verification.
- **Planned V2.0:** approved target capabilities not necessarily production-ready.
- **Later-stage preview:** capabilities shown to communicate the full vision but requiring dependency, provider, legal or security validation.

Never label a feature live merely because its reference screen exists. Provider integrations, digital signatures, external sends, secure uploads, payment operations, native AI and backend authorisation remain unimplemented until separately delivered and verified.

- Leak Radar has exactly three primary tabs.
- CFO Dashboard contains definitions and financial drill-down intent.
